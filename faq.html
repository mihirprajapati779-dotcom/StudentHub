Frequently Asked Questions

How can I reset my portal password?

Navigate to the login page and use the password recovery form or contact your department admin.

How do I register for campus events?

Log in to your student account, open the Events page, and submit your registration details.

Who can update student profile information?

Basic information can be edited via the Profile page; enrollment and branch changes require admin authorization.
